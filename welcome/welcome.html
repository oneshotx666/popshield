Pin SC WebGuard to your toolbar

For quick access to the popup, pin the extension so it's always one click away.

  1. Click the Extensions icon in Chrome's top-right toolbar.
  2. Find SC WebGuard in the list.
  3. Click the pin icon next to it — it'll turn blue when pinned.

The SC WebGuard icon will appear here once pinned.

What SC WebGuard blocks

SC WebGuard runs silently in the background and stops three categories of annoyances:

🚫
Popup tabs

Script-spawned tabs that open without you clicking anything — gone before you see them.

⚠️ While active on a tab, all new tabs from that page are blocked. If a site needs to open a new tab for a legitimate reason (checkout, sign-in), toggle that tab off in the Tabs panel.

🍪
Cookie banners & consent walls

GDPR popups, "Accept all" dialogs, and tracking consent overlays are removed instantly.

🔗
Clickjack ads

Invisible layers placed over content to hijack your clicks are removed before they can fire.

⚡

Turn on SC WebGuard

SC WebGuard starts disabled so you're always in control. Enable it now to start blocking — you can turn it off any time from the popup.

Per-tab control

While SC WebGuard is active on a tab, any new tabs that tab tries to open will be blocked — that's the protection working. If a site needs to open new tabs legitimately (like a payment portal or sign-in window), just flip that tab's toggle off in the Tabs panel and it'll behave normally again.
